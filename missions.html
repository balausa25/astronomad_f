Mission Details

🔭

James Webb Space Telescope

Active

Launched in December 2021, Webb orbits about 1.5 million km from Earth and observes in infrared light. Its 6.5 m gold-coated mirror collects roughly six times more light than Hubble’s, revealing early galaxies and exoplanet atmospheres.

🔴

Perseverance Mars Rover

Active

Landed in Jezero Crater in February 2021 to search for signs of ancient microbial life. It cached rock samples for a future return to Earth, carried the Ingenuity helicopter (the first powered flight on another planet) and tested oxygen production with MOXIE.

🌕

Artemis II

Completed

In April 2026 four astronauts flew around the Moon aboard Orion — the first crewed lunar flyby since Apollo. It tested the systems needed for later missions, with Artemis III planned as the next step, currently targeted for 2027.

🪐

Juno — Jupiter Orbiter

Active

Orbiting Jupiter since 2016, Juno studies its atmosphere, magnetic field and interior. It found cyclones clustered at the poles and hints that the planet’s core is “fuzzy” rather than solid.

☀️

Parker Solar Probe

Active

The first spacecraft to fly through the Sun’s corona. It samples particles and magnetic fields, improving our understanding of the solar wind and space weather, and reached about 692,000 km/h — the fastest human-made object.

🌌

Voyager 1 & 2

Active

Launched in 1977, both probes are now in interstellar space. Voyager 1 is the most distant human-made object, more than 24 billion km from Earth. Each carries a Golden Record as a message to any future discoverers.

🧊

Europa Clipper

En route

Launched in October 2024, it is travelling to Jupiter’s icy moon Europa to study its subsurface ocean and whether it could support life. Arrival is planned for 2030.

🚁

Dragonfly

Upcoming

A rotorcraft lander designed to fly across the dunes of Saturn’s moon Titan. Launch is planned for 2028.
