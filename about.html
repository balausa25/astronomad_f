Why I built Astronomad

Astronomad is for anyone who looks up at the night sky and wonders what is out there — students, space fans and the simply curious.

It pulls real pictures from NASA’s image library and presents planets, deep-space objects and missions in a fast, readable, mobile-friendly way, in both light and dark mode.
