Did You Know?

🌌 A day on Venus is longer than its year. It takes 243 Earth days to rotate once, but only 225 to orbit the Sun.
🌑 Neutron stars are unimaginably dense. A teaspoon of neutron-star material would weigh billions of tons on Earth.
🚀 There are more trees on Earth than stars in the Milky Way. About 3 trillion trees versus 100–400 billion stars.
